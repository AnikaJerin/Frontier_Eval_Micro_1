MeteoCode-Eval examines whether language models preserve the meaning of SYNOP, METAR and TAF codes. It includes 156 user-supplied reports, deterministic weather-decoding tasks, and saved responses from three models. In a 12-task real-report exploratory selection, GPT-5.4 passed 27 of 35 completed trials, GPT-6 Astra 36 of 36, and Meta Muse Wonder 34 of 36. Four GPT-5.4 failures were output-schema errors; observed decoding errors include a reversed SYNOP pressure-change sign and a scattered cloud layer treated as a ceiling. These small, correlated samples do not estimate general model reliability; individual source URLs, redistribution scope and independent meteorological review remain unresolved.
